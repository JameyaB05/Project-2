Future for Pluribus as a Show

As of the creation of this website, Pluribus has already been green-lit for season 2 from the beginning, set to release some time in 2027 or 2028 since filming could end up taking a long while. In the mean time, fans speculate on the journey of Carol Sturka and what the Hivemind is meant to represent. A few popular theories among fans include: An allegory for communism and anti-collectivism, colonization and the death of culture, capitalism and the latestage consumer, or the Rise of AI.
