Series Overview

The current story in Pluribus begin after a group of astrologists copy an RNA sequence that was broadcasted to them by some sort of alien life form. After they begin testing the strand on lab-rats, the Hivemind virus is spread to one of the doctors via an infected-rat-bat. Over the course of one month, the virus has spread across the planet, converting every human on Earth into one collective consciousness. Every human except, the main character and 12 other humans on the planet deemed as immune to the virus. As the story progresses, the view follows Carol as she navigates a world Post humanity's assimilation into the hivemind. Pluribus , was released back on November 7th, 2025 with 9 episodes released until the end of Season 1 on December 23, 2025.
